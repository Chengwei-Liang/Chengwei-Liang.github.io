My research focuses on radio observations of fast radio bursts (FRBs) and pulsars, with an emphasis on polarization and machine-learning applications.

## FRB and Pulsar Observations

I study the dynamic magneto-ionic environments of repeating FRBs, their polarization properties, and searches for radio transients. I am a member of the FAST FRB Key Science Project and ASKAP CRAFT collaborations, and I carry out observations with FAST and the Parkes (Murriyang) telescope.

## Machine Learning and Deep Learning

I develop machine-learning and deep-learning methods for FRB classification and searching pipelines, applying them to large samples of radio data.

## Selected Topics

- Dynamic environments of repeating FRBs (e.g., FRB 20180301A)
- Polarimetric differences between repeating and non-repeating FRBs
- Long-period radio pulsars and radio transients
- Plasma lensing in FRBs
