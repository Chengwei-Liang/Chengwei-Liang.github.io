Hi! I am **Chengwei Liang**, a senior Astronomy undergraduate at Guangzhou University and a member of FAST FRB KSP and ASKAP CRAFT Collaborations, with three years of research experience in **fast radio bursts (FRBs)**. I have conducted observations using **FAST** and **Parkes (Murriyang)**, including over 100 hours of observing time as PI. My research focuses on FRB and Pulsar observations, as well as using ML/DL for FRB search and classification.
